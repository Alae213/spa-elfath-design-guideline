Photography is evidence. El Fath sells real factories, real lines, real output — so every photo must be a real El Fath site. A picture that could be any factory anywhere says nothing about this group.

<SectionHeading id="real-only" number="01" title="Real Photos Only" />

Only photographs taken on El Fath sites ship in El Fath interfaces. Stock libraries, AI-generated scenes, and renders presented as photographs are forbidden — a reader who recognizes a stock photo stops believing the caption next to it:

<SectionHeading id="text-on-image" number="04" title="Text on Image" />

White text sits on photographs only through the production grade below — a black overlay, never blue. The grade guarantees the contrast floor; the thresholds still apply and are still tested:

<ImageDemo label="Graded caption zone" ratio="21 / 9" ratioTag="21:9" overlay={true} caption="Real lines, real output — since 1984" note="The exact hero grade with a white headline; the floor every caption stands on." />

<p className="mt-3 text-[13px] leading-6 text-[var(--apple-text)]"><span className="font-semibold">Contrast thresholds hold on photos too.</span> Body text keeps 4.5:1, large text 3:1 (WCAG AA) — measured against the lightest area the text touches, not the darkest.</p>

<p className="mt-3 text-[13px] leading-6 text-[var(--apple-text)]"><span className="font-semibold">The logo never sits on a photo.</span> Wordmark placement follows § Branding: official colors on white ground — a photograph is never that ground.</p>
