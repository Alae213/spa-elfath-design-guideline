The logo is the face of the group: a designed wordmark that merges the Arabic name «الفتح» and the Latin «SPA elFath» in a single lockup in two fixed blues. The rules in this reference are binding on every medium, and the logo is always shown in its official colors on a white ground — the theme (dark/light) never changes it on any page of this guide.

<SectionHeading id="present" number="01" title="The Logo" />

The horizontal lockup is the default for web, apps, and correspondence. It is pulled only from the official master file, never redrawn in typeset:
